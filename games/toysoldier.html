About the game

Welcome to Toy Soldier Ragdoll, a cheerful toy box physics playground sandbox for kids of all ages!

Our lime green jelly buddy with the red bandana is a wind-up tin toy soldier in this game, with a tall red hat and gold band, a red tunic with shiny buttons, blue trousers and a turning wind-up key on his back. Drag him, fling him, bounce him on the trampoline and watch him flop, wobble and pop right back up with a big smile. Nobody ever gets hurt: bumps only make cartoon stars and giggles.

Boom the bouncy toy drum, start the toy train and watch it puff along, tap the wind-up car to wind it and zoom, stack colorful wooden building blocks, spin the spinning top and wave the parade flag. Rubber chickens, tickle feathers, water balloons, trampolines and rockets are in the toy chest too.

Play with the whole tin toy crew: the toy soldier, Drummy, Twirl, Tinbot the tin robot, Jolly, Rocky the rocking horse and Squeak the wind-up mouse.
