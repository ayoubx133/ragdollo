About the game

Welcome to Astronaut Ragdoll Moon, a free play ragdoll playground sandbox for all ages!

Our lime green jelly buddy is ready for lift off in a round white space helmet with a clear visor and glowing blue lights, a white space suit with a control panel and a backpack, and blue gloves. Grab him, fling him, float him in moon gravity and watch him wobble, see cartoon stars and pop right back up with a smile. Nobody ever gets hurt here: every bonk is soft and silly.

Space toys to play with:

A moon buggy that drives around on bouncy wheels

A toy jetpack (tie it to a friend with the rope and fly)

Floating satellites that beep and spin their solar panels

Space blocks with planets, stars and moons

A big low gravity bubble that makes friends float

Comet balls that zoom with a sparkly tail

A toy rocket

Plus soft bonk toys like the rubber chicken, tickle feather, foam bat, boing glove and water balloons, and playground classics: trampoline, seesaw, rope swing, pillows, magnet, balloons and bubbles.

Five space maps to explore: the Moon with floaty gravity, a Space Station with zero gravity, the Rocket Pad, a purple Asteroid and the Mars Base.

Meet the space friends: Zip the alien, Nova the astronaut, Blip, Robo the robot, Luna and Comet the space pup. Use the tools to grab, pin, tie with rope, freeze and poof, undo anything and save your favorite scenes. Dreamy twinkly space music, no timers, no rules, just giggles.

  • Free play ragdoll sandbox starring the green buddy in an astronaut costume
  • 7 space toys plus soft bonk toys and playground classics, all kid friendly
  • 5 space maps with moon and zero gravity, from the Moon to Mars, with save slots
  • Fling, bounce and giggle with real ragdoll physics
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
