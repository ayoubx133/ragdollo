About the game

Welcome to Farm Animals Ragdoll, a cheerful physics playground sandbox for kids of all ages!

Our lime green jelly buddy puts on his straw farmer hat, with a little chick sitting on top, and his denim overalls. Drag him, fling him, bounce him on the trampoline and watch him wobble, flop and pop right back up with a big smile. Nobody ever gets hurt: bumps only make cartoon stars and giggles.

Play with floppy farm animals on four wobbly legs: Moomoo the cow, Oinky the pig, Fluffy the sheep and Penny the pony, plus Daisy the farm buddy. Drive the tractor, stack hay bales, splash in the squishy mud slime, spill the milk pail, roll pumpkins, build fences and play with peeping chicks.
