About the game

Welcome to Spooky Bones Ragdoll, a free play ragdoll playground sandbox for all ages, cute and never scary!

Our lime green jelly buddy is dressed up for a costume party: friendly skeleton face paint, a tiny tilted top hat with a red band, a black bowtie and a skeleton suit with painted bones. Grab him, fling him, bounce him on the cobweb trampoline and watch him wobble, see cartoon stars and pop right back up with a big smile. Nobody ever gets hurt here: every bonk is soft and silly.

Spooky toys to play with:

A bone xylophone that plays a tune when anything lands on it

Smiling pumpkins that roll, bounce and light up

Bat balloons that flap their wings

A cozy sleepy bed that sings a lullaby and puts friends to nap

A springy cobweb trampoline with a tiny friendly spider

A candy bucket that pops out a shower of treats

Plus soft bonk toys like the rubber chicken, tickle feather, foam bat, boing glove and water balloons, and playground classics: trampoline, seesaw, rope swing, pillows, slime, fan, bubbles and a bouncy castle.

Five spooky maps to explore: the Haunted House, a Graveyard Party with bunting and lanterns, the Pumpkin Patch at sunset, the candle lit Crypt and Moon Night with floaty gravity.

Meet the costume friends: Boney the skeleton kid, Ghosty, Pumpy the pumpkin, Batsy, Winnie the little witch, Kitty the black cat and Skelly the pup. Use the tools to grab, pin, tie with rope, freeze and poof, undo anything and save your favorite scenes. Goofy xylophone shuffle music, no timers, no rules, just giggles.

  • Free play ragdoll sandbox starring the green buddy in a cute skeleton costume
  • 6 spooky toys plus soft bonk toys and playground classics, all kid friendly
  • 5 cute spooky maps from the Haunted House to Moon Night, with save slots
  • Fling, bounce and giggle with real ragdoll physics
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
