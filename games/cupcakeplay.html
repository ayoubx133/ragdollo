About the game

Welcome to the sweetest playground in town! Our green jelly buddy wears a giant cupcake hat with pink frosting, rainbow sprinkles and a cherry on top, plus a baker apron, and plays with a crowd of candy friends: a lollipop buddy, a soft serve buddy, a chocolate sundae, a donut, a macaron, a marshmallow pup and a gumdrop.

This is a playground sandbox with no levels and no rules. Grab a buddy and bounce him on a candy cane spring, launch him up with the sprinkle popper, stack wobbly jelly cubes or plop him into sticky frosting slime. Every bump is soft: buddies see cartoon stars, giggle and pop right back up.

SWEET TOYS: sprinkle popper, rolling donuts, candy cane spring, frosting slime, spinning lollipops, wobbly jelly cubes, balloons and water balloons.

PLAYGROUND: trampoline, bouncy castle, seesaw, rope swing, bubble machine, fan, foam bat, rubber chicken, boing glove and pillows.

FIVE PLACES: Bakery, Candy Land with a rainbow, Ice Cream Shop, Chocolate Hill and the floaty Sugar Castle.

TOOLS: grab, pin, rope, freeze and poof, plus undo, slow motion, moon and zero gravity, and three save slots.

Kid friendly for all ages, no violence, just sweets and giggles. Works offline.
