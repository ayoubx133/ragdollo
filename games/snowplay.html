About the game

Welcome to Snowman Ragdoll Winter, a free play ragdoll playground sandbox for all ages!

Our lime green jelly buddy is dressed for the snow in a red knit beanie with a fluffy pompom, a red and white striped scarf, a cozy blue puffer jacket and red mittens. Grab him, fling him, send him down the ice slide on a sled and watch him wobble, see cartoon stars and pop right back up with a smile. Nobody ever gets hurt here: every bonk is soft and silly.

Winter toys to play with:

Snowballs that grow bigger as they roll across the snow

A wooden sled on slippery runners (tap it for a push)

A giant ice slide that makes everything zoom

A snow fort that tosses soft snowballs when you tap it

Hot cocoa mugs with marshmallows that warm up frozen friends

Slippery ice cubes that tinkle

Plus soft bonk toys like the rubber chicken, tickle feather, foam bat, boing glove and water balloons, and playground classics: trampoline, seesaw, rope swing, pillows, fan, bubbles, balloons and a bouncy castle.

Five winter maps to explore: Snow Hill, the Frozen Lake at sunset, a cozy Ski Lodge, a sparkly Ice Cave and the Northern Lights.

Meet the winter friends: Frosty the snowman, Pippa the penguin, Polar the bear cub, Renny the reindeer, Mitts, Skip and a fluffy baby Yeti. Use the tools to grab, pin, tie with rope, freeze and poof, undo anything and save your favorite scenes. Jingly sleigh bell music, no timers, no rules, just giggles.

  • Free play ragdoll sandbox starring the green buddy in a cozy winter costume
  • 6 snowy toys plus soft bonk toys and playground classics, all kid friendly
  • 5 winter maps from Snow Hill to the Northern Lights, with save slots
  • Fling, bounce and giggle with real ragdoll physics
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
