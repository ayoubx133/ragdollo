About the game

Welcome to the dojo! Our lime green jelly buddy is dressed up as a little blue ninja, with his red bandana tied over the hood, and he is ready to play. This is a ragdoll playground sandbox for kids: open the toy box, drop in buddies and toys, then grab, fling, pin, tie and freeze whatever you like. Bounce on the big taiko drum, wobble the padded practice dummy, ring the gong, cross the wobbly rope bridge, lean bamboo poles, toss plush foam stars and float paper lanterns. Meet the hooded trainees, the bearded sensei, Bao the panda and the ninja pup. Play in 5 places: the Dojo hall, the Bamboo Forest, sunset Rooftops, the Cherry Garden and the floaty Sky Temple. Every bump is soft and silly: buddies see cartoon stars, giggle and pop right back up. No timers, no scores, just free play. Save your favorite scenes in 3 slots.

  • Ragdoll playground sandbox with a costumed ninja buddy
  • 30 toys and buddies including a taiko drum, rope bridge and foam stars
  • 5 dojo maps, floaty gravity, slow motion and 3 save slots
  • Fling, bounce and giggle with real ragdoll physics
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
