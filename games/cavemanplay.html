About the game

Step into the Caveman Ragdoll Stone Age, a free play ragdoll playground sandbox for kids and grown ups!

Our lime green jelly buddy is dressed as a friendly cave kid with messy hair, a leopard print headband with a bone clip and a cozy fur tunic. Toss him, flip him, bounce him into a leaf pile and watch him wobble, see cartoon stars and pop back up with a grin. Every bonk is soft and silly, nobody ever gets hurt.

Stone age toys:

Big stone wheels that roll and rumble

Mammoth balloons that float and toot

A bone seesaw on a rock

Rock blocks and long stone slabs to stack

Glow stones that shine in five colors

A springy leaf pile that sends leaves flying

Plus soft bonk toys like the rubber chicken, tickle feather, foam bat and water balloons, and playground classics: trampoline, bouncy castle, springs, rope swing, fan, slime and bubbles.

Five maps: a cozy Cave with crystals and wall paintings, the Stone Village, a friendly Volcano, the snowy Ice Age and a prehistoric Jungle.

Play with the tribe: Bobo, Zuzu, Toko, Kika, Momo the baby mammoth and Tiggy the kitten. Grab, pin, tie with rope, freeze and poof, undo anything and save your scenes. A bouncy drum and marimba soundtrack, no timers, no rules, just giggles.

  • 5 prehistoric maps from the crystal cave to the jungle, with save slots

  • Plays fully offline
