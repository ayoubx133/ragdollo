About the game

Welcome to Clown Ragdoll Circus, a free play ragdoll playground sandbox for all ages!

Our lime green jelly buddy is the happiest clown in town: a curly rainbow wig, a tiny polka dot party hat with a pompom, his red bandana, a big red nose, a sunny yellow suit with pompom buttons, a rainbow ruffle collar and giant floppy red shoes. Grab him, fling him across the ring, bounce him on the circus drum and watch him wobble, see cartoon stars and pop right back up with a smile. Nobody ever gets hurt here: every bonk is soft and silly.

Circus toys to play with:

A self balancing unicycle that rides around the ring with a friend on top

A confetti cannon: tap it for a big BOOM of confetti that sends a friend flying

A swinging trapeze high under the tent

Juggling balls that hop up when you tap them

A big striped star ball to roll and balance on

A bouncy circus drum

Plus soft bonk toys like the rubber chicken, tickle feather, foam bat, boing glove and water balloons, and playground classics: trampoline, bouncy castle, seesaw, spring, rope swing, pillows, balloons, bubbles and a toy rocket.

Five circus maps to explore: the striped Big Top, the Circus Ring with a cheering crowd, a cozy Backstage, the sunny Fairground with a turning ferris wheel and the starry Night Show.

Meet the circus friends: Ringo the ringmaster, Strongo, Tutu the acrobat, Bobo the clown, Ellie the elephant and Fifi the poodle. Use the tools to grab, pin, tie with rope, freeze and poof, undo anything and save your favorite scenes. Bouncy oom pah circus music, no timers, no rules, just giggles.

  • Free play ragdoll sandbox starring the green buddy in a rainbow clown costume
  • 6 circus toys like the unicycle, confetti cannon and trapeze, plus soft bonk toys
  • 5 circus maps from the Big Top to the Night Show, with save slots
  • Fling, bounce and giggle with real ragdoll physics
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
