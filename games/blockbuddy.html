About the game

Welcome to Block Buddy Ragdoll, a chunky physics playground sandbox for kids of all ages!

Our lime green jelly buddy with the red bandana is built out of little cubes in this game. Drag him, fling him, bounce him on the spring crate and watch his blocky body flop, tumble and pop right back up with a big square grin. Nobody ever gets hurt: bumps only make cartoon stars and giggles.

Build towers from grass, stone, wood, gold and glass blocks, swim through a floating water block, launch friends with the spring crate, give the minecart a push, lean the ladder on a wall and bounce the jelly cube sky high. Rubber chickens, tickle feathers, water balloons, trampolines and rockets are in the block box too.

Play with the whole block crew: Blocky the jelly buddy, Miner, Rosie, Mossy, Goldy, Wooly the sheep and a blocky pup.
