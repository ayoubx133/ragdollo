About the game

Welcome to Cowboy Ragdoll Ranch, a free play ragdoll playground sandbox for all ages!

Our lime green jelly buddy is the friendliest cowboy on the ranch: a big brown cowboy hat with a studded band and a gold star badge, his red bandana, a checkered shirt with a brown vest and a sheriff star, blue jeans and boots with jingly star spurs. Grab him, fling him over the fence, bounce him on the barrels and watch him wobble, see cartoon stars and pop right back up with a smile. Nobody ever gets hurt here: every bonk is soft and silly.

Ranch toys to play with:

A rocking horse that rocks and neighs when you tap it

A lasso post: tap it and a rope loop catches the nearest friend

Round hay rolls to push across the ranch

A cuddly plush cactus with a flower, soft as a pillow

A covered wagon that rolls along by itself

Bouncy barrels that hop with a BOING

Plus soft bonk toys like the rubber chicken, tickle feather, foam bat, boing glove and water balloons, and playground classics: trampoline, seesaw, spring, rope swing, balls, balloons, pillows, fan and bubbles.

Five western maps to explore: the sunny Ranch with a red barn and windmill, a Desert Town with a water tower, the Saloon Stage, a red rock Canyon and the Rodeo under twinkling lights.

Meet the ranch friends: Daisy the cowgirl, the Sheriff, Dusty, Banjo, Pony the horse and Moo the cow. Use the tools to grab, pin, tie with rope, freeze and poof, undo anything and save your favorite scenes. Toe tapping banjo and harmonica music, no timers, no rules, just giggles.

  • Free play ragdoll sandbox starring the green buddy in a big cowboy hat
  • 6 ranch toys like the rocking horse, lasso post and covered wagon, plus soft bonk toys
  • 5 western maps from the Ranch to the Rodeo, with save slots
  • Fling, bounce and giggle with real ragdoll physics
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
