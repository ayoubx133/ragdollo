A farm ragdoll playground sandbox! Bounce on a straw nest, hatch chicks from egg cups and fling silly farm buddies.

About the game

Welcome to a sunny farm playground full of eggs, chicks and giggles! Our green jelly buddy wears a cracked eggshell hat with a tiny chick peeking out, a yellow shirt and blue farm overalls, and plays with a coop of soft, silly friends: a chick, a hen, a rooster, two painted easter eggs, a piglet and a fluffy lamb.

This is a playground sandbox with no levels and no rules. Grab a buddy and fling him onto the straw nest bounce, boing him off the frying pan bounce pad, tie chick balloons to a friend and watch him float away, or tap an egg cup to hatch a peeping chick. Every bump is soft: buddies see cartoon stars, giggle and pop right back up.

FARM TOYS: nest bounce, chick balloons, pan bounce, egg cups that hatch, rolly eggs, feather pillows that burst into feathers, hay bales, rubber chicken, tickle feather and water balloons.

PLAYGROUND: trampoline, seesaw, spring, rope swing, bubble machine, foam bat, boing glove, ball, pillows and a toy car.

FIVE PLACES: Hen House, Breakfast Table, Farm Yard with a turning windmill, Easter Garden and a tiled Kitchen.

TOOLS: grab, pin, rope, freeze and poof, plus undo, slow motion, moon and zero gravity, and three save slots.

Kid friendly for all ages, no violence, just eggs, chicks and giggles. Works offline.
