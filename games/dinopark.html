A dinosaur ragdoll playground sandbox! Bounce on a friendly volcano, hatch dino eggs and fling baby dinos in a plush jungle park.

About the game

Welcome to a soft and silly dinosaur park where you make the rules! Our green jelly buddy pulls on his dino hoodie, with goggly eyes, little teeth and orange spikes, and joins a gang of chubby baby dinosaurs.

This is a playground sandbox with no levels. Grab any buddy and fling him, bounce him off a puffing volcano pad, flip him with the fern catapult or balance him on a giant bone seesaw. Tap a dino egg three times and a baby dino hatches! Every bump is soft and funny: buddies see cartoon stars, giggle and stomp right back up.

DINO TOYS: dino eggs that hatch, volcano bounce pad with rainbow steam, bone seesaw, fern catapult, gooey tar slime, foam bat, rubber chicken, tickle feather, water balloon and boing glove.

PLAYGROUND: trampoline, spring, rope swing, balls, beach ball, blocks, plank, pillows, balloons and a bubble machine.

FIVE PREHISTORIC PLACES: Jungle, Volcano, Fossil Dig, Swamp and Ice Age.

TOOLS: grab, pin, rope, freeze and poof, plus undo, slow motion, moon and zero gravity, and three save slots.

Meet the herd: Rexy, Trike, Steggy, Bronto, Pip, Dina and the little hatchlings. Kid friendly for all ages, no violence, just stomps and giggles. Works offline.
