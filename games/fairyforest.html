About the game

Flutter into a magical forest ragdoll playground sandbox! Our lime green jelly buddy is a little fairy with a flower crown, tiny sparkly wings and a petal tunic, and he plays with his woodland friends: a pixie with butterfly wings, a ladybug, a mushroom gnome, a bumble bee and a bunny. Drag them, fling them and watch them wobble, see cartoon stars and pop right back up with a smile. Bounce on the mushroom trampoline and the springy daisy, pop shiny dew drops, surf a leaf glider, follow the friendly glowing fireflies, blow dandelion seeds into the air and toss bouncy acorns. Play in 5 maps: the Fairy Forest, a Mushroom Village, a giant Flower Meadow, a twilight Crystal Pond and the floaty Moon Glade. Pin, tie with ropes, freeze, poof, slow motion, gravity switch, undo and 3 save slots, with a skipping glockenspiel and flute soundtrack. Soft toys only, made for kids of all ages.

  • Fairy forest ragdoll playground sandbox with a costumed fairy buddy and 5 woodland friends
  • 20 toys incl. mushroom trampoline, flower spring, dew drops, fireflies, leaf glider and dandelions
  • 5 storybook maps incl. a floaty moon glade, glockenspiel music, save slots and undo
  • Fling, bounce and giggle with real ragdoll physics
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
