About the game

Welcome to the Ghost Ragdoll Mansion, a cute (never scary) free play ragdoll playground sandbox for all ages!

Our lime green jelly buddy is wearing a bedsheet ghost costume with a smiley ghost face, a fluttering sheet cape and purple boots. Grab him, fling him, bounce him on the cobweb trampoline and watch him wobble, see cartoon stars and pop right back up laughing. Every bonk is soft and silly, nobody ever gets hurt.

Mansion toys:

Floaty furniture: an armchair and a tea table that hover and bob

Glow candles with a soft magic light (no flames)

A bouncy cobweb trampoline with a tiny friendly spider

Rolling pumpkins with happy painted smiles

Portraits whose eyes follow your buddies (tap one and it winks)

Little Boo, a tiny ghost that floats around and says BOO!

Plus soft bonk toys like the rubber chicken, tickle feather, foam bat and water balloons, and playground classics: trampoline, seesaw, springs, rope swing, fan, slime and bubbles.

Five cozy maps: the Mansion under a big moon, the Attic, the Ballroom, the Garden Maze and the Moon Tower with floaty gravity.

Play with Boo and Peeka the little sheet ghosts, Winnie, Pumpkin, Batty and Midnight the kitty. Grab, pin, tie with rope, freeze and poof, undo anything and save your scenes. A sneaky cute swing soundtrack, no timers, no rules, just giggles.

  • Floaty furniture, glow candles, cobweb trampoline, pumpkins, portraits and a little boo ghost
  • 5 cozy night maps from the Mansion to the Moon Tower, with save slots
  • Fling, bounce and giggle with real ragdoll physics
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
