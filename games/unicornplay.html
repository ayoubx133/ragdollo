About the game

Welcome to Unicorn Ragdoll Rainbow, a sparkly free play ragdoll playground sandbox for all ages!

Our lime green jelly buddy wears a golden unicorn horn, fluffy ears, a rainbow mane and a rainbow tail. Grab him, toss him, send him down the rainbow slide and watch him wobble, giggle and pop right back up. Everything is soft and silly, nobody ever gets hurt.

Rainbow toys:

A big rainbow slide with a ladder

Cloud trampolines that float where you put them

Star wands that leave a sparkle trail (tap for magic)

Bouncy wrapped candies

Heart balloons that pop into hearts

Plus soft bonk toys like the rubber chicken, tickle feather, foam bat and water balloons, and playground classics: trampoline, bouncy castle, seesaw, springs, rope swing, fan, slime and bubbles.

Five dreamy maps: Rainbow Land, the Cloud Castle, the Candy Meadow, Star Night with floaty gravity and the Crystal Hill waterfall.

Play with Pippa and Coco the pastel ponies, Skye, Daisy, Nova and Bluebell. Grab, pin, tie with rope, freeze and poof, undo anything and save your favorite scenes. Sparkly pop music, no timers, no rules, just giggles.

  • Rainbow slide, cloud trampolines, star wands, candies and heart balloons plus soft bonk toys
  • 5 pastel maps from Rainbow Land to Star Night, with save slots
  • Fling, bounce and giggle with real ragdoll physics
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
