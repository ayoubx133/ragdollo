About the game

Welcome to a sunny ocean ragdoll playground sandbox! Our lime green jelly buddy wears a cuddly pink octopus hat that hugs his head with curly tentacles, plus a striped swim tee, and plays with his sea friends: a crab buddy with soft round claws, a squid, a seal pup, a starfish kid and a surfer in a sun hat. Drag them, fling them and watch them wobble, see cartoon stars and pop right back up with a smile. Stand on the water jet and get launched by a fountain, bump the sand castle until it squishes flat and builds itself back up, wind up the walking crab, stick a squishy starfish anywhere, toot the conch horn to start a dance party and bounce the swim ring sky high. Play in 5 maps: the Ocean Floor, sunset Tide Pools with a lighthouse, a turquoise Lagoon, a glowing Sea Cave and a floaty Sunken City. Pin, tie with ropes, freeze, poof, slow motion, gravity switch, undo and 3 save slots, with a jolly sea shanty soundtrack. Soft toys only, made for kids of all ages.

  • Ocean ragdoll playground sandbox with a buddy in an octopus hat and 5 sea friends
  • 20 toys incl. water jet, squishy sand castle, wind-up crab, sticky starfish and conch horn
  • 5 maps from tide pools to a floaty sunken city, sea shanty music, save slots and undo
  • Fling, bounce and giggle with real ragdoll physics
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
