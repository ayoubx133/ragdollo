About the game

Meow! Our lime green jelly buddy put on an orange cat ear headband over his red bandana, drew on whiskers and zipped up a cozy tabby onesie with a curly tail. This is a ragdoll playground sandbox for kids: open the kitty toy box, drop in cats, friends and toys, then grab, fling, pin, tie and freeze whatever you like. Roll the yarn balls and watch the cats trot after them, wind up the toy mouse, find the kitten hiding in the cardboard box, climb the cat tower, swat the scratch post toy and give the cats a catnip fish for hearts. Meet Ginger, Mochi, Pepper, Mimi, Lulu and Nana. Play in 5 places: the Living Room, the Rooftops, the Garden, the Cat Cafe and the floaty Night Alley. Every bump is soft and silly: buddies see cartoon stars, giggle and pop right back up. No timers, no scores, just free play, plus 3 slots to save your favorite scenes.

  • Ragdoll playground sandbox with a costumed cat buddy and real kitties
  • 30 toys and friends including yarn balls, a wind-up mouse and a cat tower
  • 5 cozy cat maps, floaty night gravity, slow motion and 3 save slots
  • Fling, bounce and giggle with real ragdoll physics
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
