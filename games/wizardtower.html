About the game

Step into a magical ragdoll playground sandbox! Our lime green jelly buddy is a little wizard with a tall blue hat full of yellow stars and moons, a starry robe and a purple cape, and he plays with his tower friends: an apprentice, a wise owl, a baby dragon with soft wings, an old sage with a fluffy beard and a black cat. Drag them, fling them and watch them wobble, see cartoon stars and pop right back up with a smile. Touch a spell bubble and float like a feather, ride the flying broom, open the magic portals and pop out on the other side, bounce in the bubbling cauldron, tap the flying book for a TA-DA of stars and change the colour of the sticky potion slime. Play in 5 maps: the Wizard Tower, a cozy Library, a rainbow Magic Garden, a sparkly Crystal Cave and the floaty Starry Sky. Pin, tie with ropes, freeze, poof, slow motion, gravity switch, undo and 3 save slots, with a playful harpsichord soundtrack. Soft toys only, made for kids of all ages.

  • 5 magical maps incl. a floaty starry sky, harpsichord music, save slots and undo
  • Fling, bounce and giggle with real ragdoll physics
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
