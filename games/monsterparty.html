About the game

It is party time! Our lime green jelly buddy put on a fuzzy purple monster hood with candy corn horns, googly eyes and soft felt teeth, right over his red bandana. This is a ragdoll playground sandbox for kids: open the monster toy box, drop in friendly monsters and toys, then grab, fling, pin, tie and freeze whatever you like. Pop the party poppers for a rain of confetti, bounce on the bubbly slime pot, roll the bouncy pumpkins, float the balloon monsters, wobble the jelly cubes and switch on the disco ball lights. Meet the gang: one eyed Bloop, Fangy, tall Ziggy, fluffy Momo and Gooby the monster pet. Play in 5 places: the Monster House, the Party Hall, the Spooky Woods, the Candy Yard and the floaty Slime Lab. Nothing is scary and every bump is soft and silly: monsters see cartoon stars, giggle and pop right back up. No timers, no scores, just free play, plus 3 slots to save your favorite scenes.

  • Ragdoll playground sandbox with a costumed monster buddy
  • 29 toys and monsters including party poppers, a slime pot and a disco ball
  • 5 cute monster maps, floaty lab gravity, slow motion and 3 save slots
  • Fling, bounce and giggle with real ragdoll physics
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
