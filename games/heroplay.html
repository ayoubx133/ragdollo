About the game

Welcome to Superhero Ragdoll City, a free play ragdoll playground sandbox for all ages!

Our lime green jelly buddy suits up as a city hero: a red eye mask, his red bandana with a shiny gold star, a blue hero suit with a star badge, a flowing red cape, red gloves and boots. Grab him, fling him across the sky, bounce him on the hero trampoline and watch him wobble, see cartoon stars and pop right back up with a smile. Nobody ever gets hurt here: every bonk is soft and silly.

City toys to play with:

City blocks that light up their windows when you tap them

A zippy hero car with star wheels

A star spangled hero trampoline for super high jumps

Comic POW clouds that pop out words like ZAP, ZOOM and WOW when anything bumps them

Star balloons that lift your heroes into the air

A fire hydrant fountain that splashes everyone up into the sky

Plus soft bonk toys like the rubber chicken, tickle feather, foam bat, boing glove and water balloons, and playground classics: seesaw, spring, rope swing, pillows, balls, bubbles and a toy rocket.

Five city maps to explore: sunny City Rooftops, a busy Street full of shops, the secret Hero Base with floaty flying gravity, the big red Bridge over the bay and Night City under the hero signal.

Meet the hero team: Bolt, Pixie, Robo, Maxi and Super Pup. Use the tools to grab, pin, tie with rope, freeze and poof, undo anything and save your favorite scenes. Bold brass march music, no timers, no rules, just giggles.

  • Free play ragdoll sandbox starring the green buddy in a caped superhero costume
  • 6 city hero toys plus soft bonk toys and playground classics, all kid friendly
  • 5 city maps from the Rooftops to Night City, with save slots
  • Fling, bounce and giggle with real ragdoll physics
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
