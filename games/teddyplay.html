A cosy toy room ragdoll playground sandbox! Bounce on a bouncy bed, ride a rocking horse and play with plush friends.

About the game

Step into a cosy toy room where every toy comes to life! Our green jelly buddy wears a stitched teddy bear hood with round ears and a cuddly teddy onesie with a little tail, and plays with a whole shelf of plush friends with button eyes: a bunny, a ducky, an elephant, a rag doll, a puppy, a kitty and a mini teddy.

This is a playground sandbox with no levels and no rules. Grab a plush and fling it onto the bouncy bed, ride the rocking horse, drum on the toy drum, roll a yarn ball or wind up the music box and watch everybody get sleepy to the lullaby. Every bump is soft: friends see cartoon stars, giggle and pop right back up.

TOY ROOM TOYS: toy train that puffs and toots, music box with a lullaby, yarn balls, rocking horse, toy drum, bouncy bed, ABC blocks, pillows and balloons.

PLAYGROUND: trampoline, bouncy castle, seesaw, rope swing, spring, bubble machine, tickle feather, rubber chicken, foam bat and boing glove.

FIVE PLACES: Toy Room, Bedroom at night, Toy Shop, Tea Party and floaty Dream Land.

TOOLS: grab, pin, rope, freeze and poof, plus undo, slow motion, moon and zero gravity, and three save slots.

Kid friendly for all ages, no violence, just soft toys and giggles. Works offline.
