About the game

Welcome to Yeti Ragdoll Mountain, a cozy physics playground sandbox for kids of all ages!

Our lime green jelly buddy pulls on a fluffy white yeti hood with two curly horns, a warm fur suit and his red scarf. Drag him, fling him, send him down the hill on a sled and watch him wobble, flop and pop right back up with a big smile. Nobody ever gets hurt: bumps only make cartoon stars and giggles.

Ride the ski lift across the sky, warm up in the steamy hot spring where everybody gets cozy, slide on slippery ice blocks, throw soft snowballs that go poof and wiggle the wobbly snowman. Trampolines, seesaws, bouncy castles and bubbles are in the toy box too.

Play with the yeti pals: Snowball, Poppy, Flurry and Mitten the baby yetis, and Billy the mountain goat.

FEATURES
