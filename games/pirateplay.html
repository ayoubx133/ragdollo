About the game

All aboard! Our lime green jelly buddy is the captain today, in a black pirate hat with a gold star, his red bandana, a gold earring and a striped shirt. This is a ragdoll playground sandbox for kids: open the treasure box, drop in the crew and toys, then grab, fling, pin, tie and freeze whatever you like. Fire the soft ball cannon, open the treasure chest full of gold, roll the barrels, spin the ship wheel, nap in the hammock and watch the friendly parrot fly around saying ahoy. Meet the crew: Matey, Skipper, Pearl, Big Bo and Shelly the sea turtle. Play in 5 places: the Ship Deck, Treasure Island, the Crystal Cave, the Harbor and the floaty Stormy Sea. Every bump is soft and silly: buddies see cartoon stars, giggle and pop right back up. No timers, no scores, just free play, plus 3 slots to save your favorite scenes.

  • Ragdoll playground sandbox with a costumed pirate captain buddy
  • 29 toys and crew mates including a soft ball cannon, treasure chest and parrot
  • 5 pirate maps, floaty storm gravity, slow motion and 3 save slots
  • Fling, bounce and giggle with real ragdoll physics
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
