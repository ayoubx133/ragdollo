About the game

Welcome to Gummy Bear Ragdoll, a sugary sweet physics playground sandbox for kids of all ages!

Our lime green jelly buddy turns into a shiny gummy candy, sparkling with sugar crystals, and still wears his red bandana. Drag him, fling him, bounce him on the chocolate spring and watch him wobble, flop and pop right back up with a big smile. Nobody ever gets hurt: bumps only make cartoon stars and giggles.

Play with a rainbow of gummy bears: Cherry, Tangy, Lemon, Berry and Bubble. Pop the lid of the candy jar, spin the swirly lollipop, scatter bouncy jelly beans, squish the sparkly sugar slime and land on soft marshmallows. Trampolines, seesaws, balloons and bubbles are in the toy box too.

FEATURES

- Open playground sandbox: no levels, no timers, just play

- The jelly buddy as a sparkly gummy candy, plus 5 colorful gummy bears

- 30 toys in total, plus Grab, Pin, Rope, Freeze and Poof tools

- 5 maps: Candy Store, Candy Factory, Jelly Lake, Sweet Mountain and Night Candy

- Slow motion, moon gravity and zero gravity

- Save your favorite play scenes in 3 slots

- Its own bubbly candy pop music
