About the game

Welcome to a playground sandbox drawn in pen and pencil! Our green jelly buddy puts on his doodle hero costume, with a scribbled ink headband, an eye mask and a red scarf, and jumps into a notebook full of silly stickman friends.

There are no levels and no rules. Grab any buddy and fling him across the page, bounce him on a trampoline, slide him down a giant pencil ramp or tie him to a balloon. Every bump is soft and funny: buddies see cartoon stars, giggle and pop right back up.

DOODLE TOYS: pencil ramp, squishy eraser, gliding paper plane, floating ink bubble, crumpled paper ball, twangy ruler, foam bat, rubber chicken, tickle feather and water balloon.

PLAYGROUND: trampoline, seesaw, spring, rope swing, boing glove, balls, blocks, pillows, fan and toy rocket.

FIVE HAND DRAWN PLACES: Notebook, Sketch City, Doodle Park, Graph Paper Lab and Chalkboard.

TOOLS: grab, pin, rope, freeze and poof, plus undo, slow motion, moon and zero gravity, and three save slots for your best scenes.

Meet the stickman gang: Penny, Ruby, Minty, Graff, Viola, Ziggy and Scribble the doodle pup. Kid friendly for all ages, no violence, just giggles. Works offline.
