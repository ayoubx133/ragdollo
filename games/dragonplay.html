About the game

Welcome to the Baby Dragon Ragdoll Lair, a friendly free play ragdoll playground sandbox for all ages!

Our lime green jelly buddy is dressed as a chubby baby dragon: a spotted dragon hood with big eyes, golden horns and spikes, little wings, a cream belly and a swishy tail. No fire here, just giggles! Grab him, toss him, bounce him on a drifting cloud and watch him wobble, see cartoon stars and pop right back up.

Dragon toys:

Gem piles that tumble and clink

Wobbly dragon eggs: tap three times and a baby dragon peeks out

A treasure chest full of shiny sparkles

Flappy wings that flutter up by themselves (tie a buddy on with the rope!)

Drifting clouds that bounce buddies sky high

Plus soft bonk toys like the rubber chicken, tickle feather, foam bat and water balloons, and playground classics: trampoline, bouncy castle, seesaw, springs, rope swing, fan and bubbles.

Five maps: the cozy Dragon Lair, the Castle, the Mountain Peak, the glowing Gem Cave and the Sky Island with floaty gravity.

Play with Rosie, Blu, Ziggy, Pip the little knight, Gemma and Nibble the hatchling. Grab, pin, tie with rope, freeze and poof, undo anything and save your scenes. A cheerful storybook march, no timers, no rules, just fun.

  • Gems, dragon eggs, treasure, flappy wings and drifting clouds plus soft bonk toys
  • 5 fairy tale maps from the Dragon Lair to the Sky Island, with save slots
  • Fling, bounce and giggle with real ragdoll physics
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
