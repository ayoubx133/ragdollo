About the game

Swim into a shiny pastel ragdoll playground sandbox! Our lime green jelly buddy becomes a merkid with a seashell tiara, a starfish clip on his red bandana, a sparkly scale top and mermaid tail fins, and he plays with his lagoon friends: a mermaid girl, a seahorse, a pearl princess, a narwhal kid and a sea otter. Drag them, fling them and watch them wobble, see cartoon stars and pop right back up with a smile. Float buddies away in a bubble ride, bounce on the giant shell trampoline, ride the squeaky dolphin float, swing on the coral swing, toss shiny pearl balls and swish through the shell harp to play real notes. Play in 5 maps: a pastel Lagoon, the floaty Coral Palace, rainbow Beach Rocks, a calm Moon Bay and a golden Treasure Cove. Pin, tie with ropes, freeze, poof, slow motion, gravity switch, undo and 3 save slots, with a dreamy harp soundtrack. Soft toys only, made for kids of all ages.

  • 20 toys incl. bubble ride, shell trampoline, dolphin float, coral swing and a playable shell harp
  • 5 dreamy maps incl. a floaty coral palace, gentle harp music, save slots and undo
  • Fling, bounce and giggle with real ragdoll physics
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
