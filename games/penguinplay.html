About the game

Brrr, it is playtime on the iceberg! Penguin Ragdoll Iceberg is a cheerful, gentle ragdoll playground sandbox for kids and grown ups. Our lime green jelly buddy zipped up his penguin hoodie, with a big orange beak and flippers, and he is ready to slide.

Open the toy box and build your own snowy fun: a slippery ice slide that sends everyone zooming, a wooden sled, snowballs that grow bigger as they roll, a cozy igloo with a little friend peeking out, flopping fish toys and a floating seal balloon. Add trampolines, seesaws, rope swings, bubble machines, toy rockets and soft toys like the water balloon, the tickle feather and the rubber chicken.

Play with lots of friends: a penguin, a fluffy chick, a polar bear cub, a happy seal and two jelly buddies in winter gear. Grab them, fling them, tie them with ropes, pin them, freeze them in the air or make things vanish with a magic poof. Every bump is soft and silly: cartoon stars, giggles and wobbles, and everybody waddles right back up with a smile.

Visit 5 places: the Iceberg, the Snow Village, the sparkly Ice Cave, the floaty Polar Sea under the ice and the Aurora Night. Switch gravity, try slow motion and save your favorite scenes in 3 slots.

No timers, no scores, just free play. Works offline.

  • Ragdoll playground sandbox with a jelly buddy in a cute penguin hoodie
  • Ice toys: ice slide, sled, growing snowballs, igloo, fish toys and a seal balloon
  • 5 snowy maps from the Iceberg to the Aurora Night, plus gravity and slow motion
  • Fling, bounce and giggle with real ragdoll physics
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
