About the game

Welcome to Knight Ragdoll Castle, a free play ragdoll playground sandbox for all ages!

Our lime green jelly buddy becomes a brave little knight: a shiny silver helmet with the visor up, a fluffy red plume, gold rivets, his red bandana peeking out, silver armor with a red tabard and a royal blue cape. Grab him, fling him over the castle walls, bounce him on the bouncy castle and watch him wobble, see cartoon stars and pop right back up with a smile. Nobody ever gets hurt here: every bonk is soft and silly.

Castle toys to play with:

A wooden horse on wheels that neighs and trots when you tap it

Castle blocks and tower blocks to stack your own fortress

A hay catapult: put a friend on the spoon, tap it and watch them fly

Soft hay bales for safe landings

A drawbridge gatehouse: tap it and the bridge swings up on its chain

A striped foam lance that only makes silly puffs

Plus soft bonk toys like the rubber chicken, tickle feather, foam bat, boing glove and water balloons, and playground classics: trampoline, bouncy castle, seesaw, rope swing, pillows, balls, balloons and bubbles.

Five castle maps to explore: the sunny Castle Yard, the golden Throne Room, the Tower Top above the clouds, the Dragon Field with a big sleepy friendly dragon and a cozy Village market.

Meet the royal friends: the Princess, the King, the Jester, the Squire, a Pony and Drago the baby dragon. Use the tools to grab, pin, tie with rope, freeze and poof, undo anything and save your favorite scenes. Merry flute and lute music, no timers, no rules, just giggles.

  • Free play ragdoll sandbox starring the green buddy in a knight helmet and cape
  • 6 castle toys like the hay catapult and the drawbridge, plus soft bonk toys
  • 5 storybook castle maps from the Throne Room to the Dragon Field, with save slots
  • Fling, bounce and giggle with real ragdoll physics
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
