About the game

Welcome to the Viking Ragdoll Village, a free play ragdoll playground sandbox for all ages!

Our lime green jelly buddy put on his soft viking helmet with round horns and yarn braids, a fur collar and a toy shield on his back. Grab him, fling him, bounce him on the big drum and watch him wobble, see cartoon stars and pop right back up with a smile. Nobody ever gets hurt here: every bonk is soft and silly.

Viking toys to play with:

Longboat with a striped sail (tap it for a horn toot)

Stackable wooden barrels

Round toy shields that roll like wheels

Snowballs that grow bigger as they roll

A wobbly rope bridge

A big bouncy drum that goes BOOM

A snow sled that slides far

Plus soft bonk toys like the rubber chicken, tickle feather, foam bat and water balloons, and playground classics: trampoline, seesaw, springs, rope swing, fan, bubbles and more.

Five maps to explore: the Viking Village, the deck of a Longboat at sea, a Snow Fjord, the Great Hall and the Northern Lights with floaty gravity.

Meet the villagers: Bjorn, Inga, Leif, Runa, Knut and Mossy the reindeer. Use the tools to grab, pin, tie with rope, freeze and poof, undo anything and save your favorite scenes. Cheerful folk music, no timers, no rules, just giggles.

  • 7 viking toys plus soft bonk toys and playground classics, all kid friendly
  • 5 viking maps from the snowy village to the Northern Lights, with save slots
  • Fling, bounce and giggle with real ragdoll physics
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
