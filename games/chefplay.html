About the game

Welcome to Chef Ragdoll Kitchen, a free play ragdoll playground sandbox for all ages!

Our lime green jelly buddy is the little head chef: a tall puffy white chef hat over his red bandana, a white chef jacket with two rows of buttons, a red neck scarf and a blue striped apron. Grab him, fling him across the kitchen, bounce him on the frying pan and watch him wobble, see cartoon stars and pop right back up with a smile. Nothing is hot and nobody ever gets hurt here: every bonk is soft and silly.

Kitchen toys to play with:

A frying pan bouncer: tap it to FLIP everything on it like a pancake

Squishy pizza dough with pepperoni or veggie toppings

A food mixer with a whirl that spins friends and fruit around

A wobbly jelly dessert topped with cream and a cherry

Giant fruit to roll: a watermelon, an orange and an apple

A soft rolling pin that only makes flour puffs

Plus soft bonk toys like the rubber chicken, tickle feather, foam bat, boing glove and water balloons, and playground classics: trampoline, seesaw, rope swing, pillows, balloons, bubbles and a bouncy castle.

Five tasty maps to explore: the sunny Kitchen, a cozy Restaurant, the Food Market, the Pizza Shop with its brick oven and the Candy Factory with lollipops and a candy conveyor.

Meet the food friends: Tommy the tomato, Berry, Pepper the waiter, Bea the baker, Muffin and Kitty the kitchen cat. Use the tools to grab, pin, tie with rope, freeze and poof, undo anything and save your favorite scenes. Swinging cooking show jazz music, no timers, no rules, just giggles.

  • 5 tasty maps from the Kitchen to the Candy Factory, with save slots
  • Fling, bounce and giggle with real ragdoll physics
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
