About the game

Time for the dog park! Our lime green jelly buddy put on a floppy eared puppy hat, tied his red bandana around his neck with a gold bone tag and zipped up a spotty puppy onesie with a waggy tail. This is a ragdoll playground sandbox for kids: open the puppy toy box, drop in dogs, friends and toys, then grab, fling, pin, tie and freeze whatever you like. Toss tennis balls and frisbees and watch the dogs run after them, squeak the bones, find the pup peeking out of the dog house, jump through the agility hoop for a cheer and splash in the sprinkler. Meet Daisy, Biscuit, Peppy, Noodle, Zoe and Sam. Play in 5 places: the Dog Park, the Backyard, the Dog Beach, the floaty Snow Park and the Dog Show. Every bump is soft and silly: buddies see cartoon stars, giggle and pop right back up. No timers, no scores, just free play, plus 3 slots to save your favorite scenes.

  • Ragdoll playground sandbox with a costumed puppy buddy and park dogs
  • 30 toys and friends including tennis balls, a frisbee and an agility hoop
  • 5 sunny puppy maps, floaty snow gravity, slow motion and 3 save slots
  • Fling, bounce and giggle with real ragdoll physics
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
