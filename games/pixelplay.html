About the game

Welcome to Pixel Ragdoll Retro, a crunchy 8-bit physics playground sandbox for kids of all ages!

Our lime green jelly buddy with the red bandana is drawn in chunky retro pixels in this game. Drag him, fling him, bounce him on the mushroom spring and watch his pixel body flop, tumble and pop right back up with a big grin. Nobody ever gets hurt: bumps only make cartoon stars and giggles.

Bonk the floating question block to pop out shiny coins, jump into the pipe and get launched sky high, stand on a smiling cloud, chase the bouncing super star and stack brick blocks. Rubber chickens, tickle feathers, water balloons, trampolines and rockets are in the pixel box too.

Play with the whole pixel crew: Pixel the jelly buddy, Byte, Pixie, Chip, Mega, Kitty the cat and Bloop.
