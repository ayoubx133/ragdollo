About the game

Welcome to the farm! Pumpkin Ragdoll Farm is a cozy, gentle ragdoll playground sandbox for kids and grown ups. Our lime green jelly buddy put on his pumpkin hat with a straw brim, a red check shirt and denim overalls, and he is ready for harvest time.

Open the toy box and build your own farm fun: a hay wagon that rolls back and forth, a wheelbarrow, an apple barrel that spills shiny apples, bouncy rolling pumpkins, a big leaf pile to jump into and a wobbly scarecrow that always stands back up and says howdy. Add trampolines, seesaws, rope swings, bubble machines, toy rockets and soft toys like the rubber chicken, the tickle feather and the foam bat.

Play with lots of friends: a friendly scarecrow buddy, a pig, a fluffy sheep, a little chick, an apple buddy and a corn buddy. Grab them, fling them, tie them with ropes, pin them, freeze them in the air or make things vanish with a magic poof. Every bump is soft and silly: cartoon stars, giggles and wobbles, and everybody pops right back up with a smile.

Visit 5 places: the sunny Farm, the Pumpkin Patch, inside the Barn, the Corn Maze and the glowing Harvest Night. Switch gravity, try slow motion and save your favorite scenes in 3 slots.

No timers, no scores, just free play. Works offline.

  • Ragdoll playground sandbox with a jelly buddy in a cute pumpkin hat
  • Farm toys: hay wagon, wheelbarrow, apple barrel, pumpkins, leaf pile and a wobbly scarecrow
  • 5 maps from the sunny Farm to the Harvest Night, plus gravity and slow motion
  • Fling, bounce and giggle with real ragdoll physics
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
