About the game

Welcome to Sock Puppet Ragdoll, a cosy physics playground sandbox for kids of all ages!

Our lime green jelly buddy with the red bandana is a striped sock puppet of himself in this game, with button eyes, felt cheeks and a floppy sock toe on his head. Drag him, fling him, bounce him on the trampoline and watch him wiggle, flop and pop right back up with a big stitched smile. Nobody ever gets hurt: bumps only make cartoon stars and giggles.

Start the washing machine for a bubbly spin cycle, hop into the laundry basket, bounce on the clothes line, roll giant buttons, chase a yarn ball that unravels behind it and toss fluffy pom poms. Rubber chickens, tickle feathers, water balloons, trampolines and rockets are in the laundry box too.

Play with the whole sock crew: Socky the jelly buddy, Dots, Argy, Stripey, Fuzz, the Sock Pup and Wormy.

FEATURES

- Open playground sandbox: no levels, no timers, just play
