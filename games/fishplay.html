About the game

Dive into a bubbly ragdoll playground sandbox! Our lime green jelly buddy suits up as a snorkel diver with a fish fin cap, a snorkel mask and orange flippers, then plays with his ocean friends: a clownfish buddy, a blue tang, a sea turtle, a spiky puffer pal and a goggle girl. Drag them, fling them, bounce them on coral springs and watch them wobble, see cartoon stars and pop right back up with a smile. Open the bubble clam and float everybody up on a column of bubbles, ride the little submarine, tickle buddies with a squishy jellyfish, bump the puffer fish until it puffs up big and round, and shake the treasure chest for a shower of shiny coins. Play in 5 underwater maps: the Aquarium, a bright Coral Reef, a friendly Shipwreck, the glowing Deep Sea with floaty water gravity and a golden Kelp Forest. Pin, tie with ropes, freeze, poof, slow motion, gravity switch, undo and 3 save slots. Soft toys only, no scary stuff, made for kids of all ages.

  • Underwater ragdoll playground sandbox with a costumed diver buddy and 5 fish friends
  • 20 toys incl. bubble clam, coral spring, submarine, jellyfish, puffer fish and treasure chest
  • 5 ocean maps with floaty deep sea water, sunny calypso music, save slots and undo
  • Fling, bounce and giggle with real ragdoll physics
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
