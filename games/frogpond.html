About the game

Hop on in, the pond is open! Frog Ragdoll Pond is a happy, gentle ragdoll playground sandbox for kids and grown ups. Our lime green jelly buddy put on his spotted frog hat with two big googly frog eyes, and he is ready to splash.

Open the toy box and make the pond your own: bouncy lily pads, a log seesaw on a mossy rock, a frog fountain whose water jet lifts everybody up, a smiling rain cloud that can make a rainbow, buzzy plush flies and a slow little snail that hides in its shell. Add trampolines, rope swings, bubble machines, toy rockets and soft toys like the water balloon, the rubber chicken and the tickle feather.

Play with lots of friends: a blue frog, an orange frog, a chubby green frog, a toad, a turtle with a shiny shell, a pink axolotl and a jelly buddy in a yellow rain hat. Grab them, fling them, tie them with ropes, pin them, freeze them in the air or make things vanish with a magic poof. Every bump is soft and silly: cartoon stars, giggles and wobbles, and everybody hops right back up with a smile.

Visit 5 places: the sunny Pond, the misty Swamp with fireflies, the leafy Rainforest, the big Waterfall with a rainbow and the cozy Rain Night. Switch gravity, try slow motion and save your favorite scenes in 3 slots.

No timers, no scores, just free play. Works offline.

  • Ragdoll playground sandbox with a jelly buddy in a cute frog hat
  • Pond toys: lily pads, log seesaw, frog fountain, rain cloud, buzzy flies and a snail
  • 5 maps from the sunny Pond to the Rain Night, plus gravity and slow motion
  • Fling, bounce and giggle with real ragdoll physics
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
