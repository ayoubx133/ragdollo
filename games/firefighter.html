About the game

Welcome to the Firefighter Ragdoll Station, a cheerful physics playground sandbox for kids of all ages!

Our lime green jelly buddy puts on his shiny red firefighter helmet and golden coat and joins the fire crew. Drag him, fling him, bounce him on the rescue net and watch him wobble, flop and pop right back up with a big smile. Nobody ever gets hurt: bumps only make cartoon stars and giggles.

Spray the water hose at splash targets, turn on the hydrant fountain and watch toys float on the water, honk the siren on the fire truck, ring the station bell and stack traffic cones and ladders. Water balloons, rubber chickens, tickle feathers and bouncy trampolines are all in the toy box too.

Play with the whole crew: Captain the jelly buddy, Pippa, Blaze, Rosie, the Chief and Sparky the dalmatian pup.

FEATURES
