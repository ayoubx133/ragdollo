About the game

Blast off to a glowing space playground where everything floats, bounces and giggles! Our green jelly buddy puts on his bubble space helmet with two springy antennae and a purple star suit, and meets a crowd of round, friendly aliens with three eyes, one big eye and wiggly antennae.

This is a playground sandbox with no levels and no rules. Grab a buddy and fling him across the moon, let the UFO lift him up in its green tractor beam, toss him into a zero gravity bubble or bounce him sky high on the star trampoline. Every bump is soft and silly: buddies see cartoon stars, giggle and float right back up.

SPACE TOYS: UFO tractor beam, floaty moon rocks, zero gravity bubble, star trampoline, wobbly jelly planets, toy rocket, foam bat, rubber chicken, tickle feather and water balloon.

PLAYGROUND: trampoline, seesaw, spring, boing glove, balls, beach ball, blocks, balloons, magnet and a bubble machine.

FIVE SPACE PLACES: Moon Base with moon gravity, Mars, Space Station, Alien Jungle and Galaxy with zero gravity.

TOOLS: grab, pin, rope, freeze and poof, plus undo, slow motion, moon and zero gravity, and three save slots.

Meet the crew: Zorp, Blip, Nova, Glorb, Kooky and Comet the space pup. Kid friendly for all ages, no violence, just floaty giggles. Works offline.
