About the game

Swing into a sunny jungle playground full of vines, bananas and giggles! Our green jelly buddy wears a cheeky monkey hood with big round ears and a banana tucked into his bandana, an explorer outfit and a curly monkey tail, and plays with jungle friends: a monkey, a parrot, a toucan, a sloth, a tiger cub, a baby elephant and a banana buddy.

This is a playground sandbox with no levels and no rules. Grab a buddy and swing him on a jungle vine, spin him on the tire swing, bounce him on the giant leaf bouncer or stack the smiling totem blocks. Every bump is soft: buddies see cartoon stars, giggle and pop right back up.

JUNGLE TOYS: jungle vines, tire swing, bananas you can peel, coconuts, smiling totem blocks, leaf bouncer and a rubber chicken.

PLAYGROUND: trampoline, seesaw, spring, bubble machine, fan, balloons, beach ball, foam bat, water balloon and boing glove.

FIVE PLACES: Jungle, floaty Treetops, Temple Ruins, River with a waterfall and Volcano Isle with a smiling volcano.

TOOLS: grab, pin, rope, freeze and poof, plus undo, slow motion, moon and zero gravity, and three save slots.

Kid friendly for all ages, no violence, just swings and giggles. Works offline.
