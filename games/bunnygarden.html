A spring garden ragdoll playground sandbox! Swing on a flower swing, bounce on mushrooms and chase butterflies.

About the game

Hop into a blooming spring garden full of flowers, carrots and giggles! Our green jelly buddy wears long white bunny ears with one floppy ear, a flowery shirt, garden boots and a fluffy bunny tail, and plays with friendly garden critters: a bunny, a ladybug, a bee, a snail, a hopping bunny, a hedgehog and a mole.

This is a playground sandbox with no levels and no rules. Grab a buddy and bounce him on the mushroom, sit him on the flower vine garden swing, sprinkle him with the watering can or let the butterflies flutter around. Every bump is soft: buddies see cartoon stars, giggle and pop right back up.

GARDEN TOYS: crunchy carrots, watering can, flower spring, garden swing, fluttering butterflies, mushroom bounce, tickle feather and water balloons.

PLAYGROUND: trampoline, seesaw, rope swing, bubble machine, fan, ball, balloons, pillows, foam bat and boing glove.

FIVE PLACES: Garden, Meadow, a cosy Burrow, Flower Field with a rainbow and a Rainy Garden.

TOOLS: grab, pin, rope, freeze and poof, plus undo, slow motion, moon and zero gravity, and three save slots.

Kid friendly for all ages, no violence, just flowers and giggles. Works offline.
