About the game

Welcome to Gingerbread Ragdoll Bakery, a free play ragdoll playground sandbox for all ages!

Our lime green jelly buddy is the head baker today: a tall puffy chef hat decorated with a gingerbread cookie and star cookies, a white baker jacket, a pink apron with a heart and pink oven mitts. Grab him, fling him, bounce him on a tray of cookies and watch him wobble, see cartoon stars and pop right back up with a smile. Nobody ever gets hurt here: every bonk is soft and silly.

Bakery toys to play with:

A stand mixer that whips up a swirly whirlwind (tap it on and off)

An oven tray trampoline full of jumping cookies

A rolling pin that rolls across the floor

Candy canes that twirl

Squishy sticky frosting blobs with sprinkles

Bouncy gumdrops

A big rolling donut

Plus soft bonk toys like the rubber chicken, tickle feather, foam bat, boing glove and water balloons, and playground classics: trampoline, seesaw, rope swing, pillows, fan, bubbles and balloons.

Five sweet maps to explore: the Bakery, a pink Candy Kitchen, Gingerbread Town, inside the Cookie Jar and the Chocolate River.

Meet the sweet friends: Ginger the gingerbread kid, Cuppy the cupcake, Minty, Muffin, Berry the strawberry, Lolly and Cookie the gingerbread pup. Use the tools to grab, pin, tie with rope, freeze and poof, undo anything and save your favorite scenes. Bouncy swing piano music, no timers, no rules, just giggles.

  • 5 sweet maps from the Bakery to the Chocolate River, with save slots
  • Fling, bounce and giggle with real ragdoll physics
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
