About the game

Welcome to Ragdoll Toy Box, a funny physics playground sandbox for kids of all ages!

Meet our lime green jelly buddy with his red bandana and his colorful jelly friends. Drag them, fling them, bounce them on the trampoline and watch them wobble, flop and pop right back up with a big smile. Nobody ever gets hurt: bumps only make cartoon stars and giggles.

Open the toy box for balls, blocks, planks, balloons that lift a buddy into the sky, a seesaw, a fan, a magnet, a spring, a rope swing, a toy car, a bouncy castle, a toy rocket with rainbow puffs and a bubble machine. Pin things to the wall, tie them with ropes, freeze them, switch to moon gravity or zero gravity and try slow motion.

Play in 5 places: the Playroom, the Backyard, the Beach, Space and Candy Land, and save your best scenes in 3 save slots.

  • Cute jelly buddies with funny faces that react to every bump
  • 18 toys and handy tools: pin, rope, freeze, undo and slow motion
  • 5 places to play and 3 save slots for your scenes
  • Fling, bounce and giggle with real ragdoll physics
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
