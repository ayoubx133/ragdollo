About the game

Welcome to the bamboo forest! Panda Ragdoll Bamboo is a silly, gentle ragdoll playground sandbox for kids and grown ups. Our lime green jelly buddy put on his cozy panda hood and panda onesie, and he is ready to play.

Open the toy box and drop in anything you like: a bouncy bamboo boing pad, a swinging hammock for sleepy naps, a squishy bao bun, rolling rice balls, glowing sky lanterns and quacking rubber ducks. Add trampolines, seesaws, rope swings, bubble machines, toy rockets and soft toys like the tickle feather, the foam bat and the rubber chicken.

Play with lots of friends: a panda cub, a big four legged panda, a fluffy red panda and three jelly buddies in bamboo land outfits. Grab them, fling them, tie them with ropes, pin them to the wall, freeze them in the air or make things vanish with a magic poof. Every bump is soft and silly: cartoon stars, giggles and wobbles, and everybody pops right back up with a smile.

Visit 5 places: the Bamboo Forest, the Panda Garden, a floaty Mountain top, the River with its waterfall and the night time Lantern Festival. Switch gravity, try slow motion and save your favorite scenes in 3 slots.

No timers, no scores, just free play. Works offline.

  • Bamboo toys: hammock, bao bun, rice balls, sky lanterns, rubber ducks and more
  • 5 maps from the Bamboo Forest to the Lantern Festival, plus gravity and slow motion
  • Fling, bounce and giggle with real ragdoll physics
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
