About the game

Welcome to the Builder Ragdoll Site, a silly physics playground sandbox for kids of all ages!

Our lime green jelly buddy puts on his yellow hard hat, orange safety vest and tool belt and gets to work. Drag him, fling him, hang him on the crane hook and watch him wobble, flop and pop right back up with a big smile. Nobody ever gets hurt: bumps only make cartoon stars and giggles.

Tie buddies and toys to the tower crane and watch the trolley carry them across the sky. Swing the big SOFT foam wrecking ball, stack toy bricks and steel beams, squish the cement slime, tip the dump truck and line up road barriers. Trampolines, seesaws, springs and magnets are in the toy box too.

Play with the whole crew: Boss the jelly buddy, Bella, Bolt, Max, Ruby and Digger the bulldog pup.
