About the game

Step into a bright robot workshop and play however you like! Our green jelly buddy suits up in a shiny robot helmet with a glowing antenna light, and his robot friends are ready to bounce, roll and beep.

This is a playground sandbox with no levels and no rules. Grab a buddy and fling him, ride him along a conveyor belt, launch him off a spring piston or let a giant gear carry him round and round. Robots are made of metal, so the magnet crane can scoop them up! Every bump is soft and silly: buddies see cartoon stars, giggle and get right back up.

LAB MACHINES: conveyor belt, spring piston, magnet crane, floating gear wheel, rainbow light gate that counts everything that passes, magnet, steel ball, toy car, toy rocket and boing glove.

PLAYGROUND: trampoline, seesaw, spring, foam bat, rubber chicken, tickle feather, water balloon, balls, blocks and a bubble machine.

FIVE ROBOT PLACES: Robot Lab, Factory, Junkyard, Space Dock with moon gravity and Neon Garage.

TOOLS: grab, pin, rope, freeze and poof, plus undo, slow motion, moon and zero gravity, and three save slots.

Meet the crew: Beep, Gigi, Tink, Zap, Nano and Rover the robo pup. Kid friendly for all ages, no violence, just beeps and giggles. Works offline.
