About the game

Put on your goggles, it is slime time! Slime Ragdoll Lab is a silly, gentle ragdoll playground sandbox for kids and grown ups. Our lime green jelly buddy pulled his lab goggles over his red bandana, slipped into a white lab coat and blue rubber gloves, and he is ready to make goo.

Open the toy box and set up your own lab: a slime maker machine that plops out bouncy goo balls, a wobbly slime pool where everything floats and giggles, test tubes with liquid that stays level, a bubbling flask that foams, and squishy foam blocks. Add trampolines, seesaws, rope swings, bubble machines, toy rockets and soft toys like the water balloon, the foam bat and the rubber chicken.

Play with lots of friends: three wobbly slime blobs, a lab mouse, a friendly lab robot and a scientist buddy with round glasses. Grab them, fling them, tie them with ropes, pin them, freeze them in the air or make things vanish with a magic poof. Every bump is soft and silly: cartoon stars, giggles and wobbles, and everybody pops right back up with a smile.

Visit 5 places: the Slime Lab, the Goo Factory, the Sewer Fun tunnels, the glowing Neon Lab and the floaty Secret Base. Switch gravity, try slow motion and save your favorite scenes in 3 slots.

No timers, no scores, just free play. Works offline.

  • Ragdoll playground sandbox with a jelly buddy in lab goggles and a lab coat
  • Lab toys: slime maker, goo balls, slime pool, test tubes, bubble flask and foam blocks
  • 5 maps from the Slime Lab to the floaty Secret Base, plus gravity and slow motion
  • Fling, bounce and giggle with real ragdoll physics
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
