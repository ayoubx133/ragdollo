About the game

Welcome to Mummy Ragdoll Pyramid, a free play ragdoll playground sandbox for all ages!

Our lime green jelly buddy is wrapped up as the cutest little mummy: soft bandages round his head and chin with his happy face peeking out, his red bandana knot tied on the side, a golden Egyptian collar and a gold belt. Grab him, fling him, send him down the sand slide and watch him wobble, see cartoon stars and pop right back up with a smile. Nobody ever gets hurt here: every bonk is soft and silly.

Pyramid toys to play with:

A golden sarcophagus toy box that opens with a TA-DA surprise

A big sand slide that makes everything zoom

Shiny scarab beetles that crawl around by themselves

Pyramid blocks to stack and topple

A bendy palm tree with waving leaves

A wiggle snake plush that wiggles along the sand

Plus soft bonk toys like the rubber chicken, tickle feather, foam bat, boing glove and water balloons, and playground classics: trampoline, seesaw, rope swing, pillows, beach ball, fan, balloons and bubbles.

Five Egyptian maps to explore: the Pyramids, the Desert dunes, a painted Tomb, a green Oasis and the Temple at sunset.

Meet the desert friends: Pharo the kid pharaoh, Wrappy the little mummy, Cleo the golden cat, Cammy the camel, Nila and Scaraby. Use the tools to grab, pin, tie with rope, freeze and poof, undo anything and save your favorite scenes. Bouncy desert drum music, no timers, no rules, just giggles.

  • 5 Egyptian maps from the Pyramids to the Temple, with save slots
  • Fling, bounce and giggle with real ragdoll physics
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
