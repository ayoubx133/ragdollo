About the game

Welcome to Paper Doll Ragdoll, a crafty physics playground sandbox for kids of all ages!

Our lime green jelly buddy with the red bandana is a paper cut-out doll in this game, with white scissor edges, sticky tape and shiny paper fasteners at his joints. Drag him, fling him, bounce him on the trampoline and watch him flop, flutter and pop right back up with a big smile. Nobody ever gets hurt: bumps only make cartoon stars and giggles.

Throw paper planes that glide across the room, tape friends together with the tape roll, sit in a cardboard box, slide giant crayons that draw colorful scribbles, flutter sticky notes with doodles and toss crumpled paper balls. Rubber chickens, tickle feathers, water balloons, trampolines and rockets are in the craft box too.

Play with the whole paper crew: the paper buddy, Snip, Dotty, Sketch, Ori, Foldy the paper pup and Inky.
